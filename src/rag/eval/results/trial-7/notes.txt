        In the Industrial Data Ontology (IDO), the concept of "Failure Effect" is represented in the context of failure modes and is associated with the consequences of a failure mode on the operation, function, or status of an item. Specifically, a "Failure Effect" is treated as an "FSO: process" that results from a "FMO: functional failure," which means it is a direct outcome of a failure mode and can be categorized as a subclass of "FunctionalFailure."
