        In the context of the Industrial Data Ontology (IDO), a "Failure Effect" can be considered as a consequence resulting from a "Failure Mode." Specifically, it is characterized as a process that corresponds to the operational, functional, or status impact of a failure (as defined in IEC 60812:2008). Within IDO, a "Failure Effect" can be related to a "Failure Event," and it exists in the hierarchy of concepts that deal with functional failures and their observation. To model this effectively, one would typically use classes within a Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA) framework aligned with IDO.
